About

Bit2Byte KUET

A student-led software research and development community focused on learning, building, sharing, and growing together.

Who We Are

Building Developers Together

Bit2Byte KUET is a software-focused community for students who want to improve their programming, development, research, project, and career skills.

The community helps members learn through workshops, team projects, hackathon preparation, technical discussions, blog writing, and peer mentoring.

Mission, Vision, Goal
